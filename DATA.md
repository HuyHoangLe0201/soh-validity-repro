# Datasets

The raw data are not redistributed here. Download each dataset from its source and cite the original publication.

| Dataset | Source | Loader / script |
|---|---|---|
| NASA PCoE Li-ion batteries (B0005, B0006, B0007, B0018) | NASA Prognostics Center of Excellence, "5. Battery Data Set" | `src/nasa_features.py`, `scripts/01_reproduce_lobo.py` |
| MATR (Severson et al. 2019, batches 1-3) | data.matr.io/1 (HDF5 batch files) | `src/matr_loader.py` |
| HUST LFP (Ma et al. 2022) | Mendeley Data, doi:10.17632/nsc7hnsg4s | `src/hust_loader.py` (restricted unpickler in `src/safe_pickle.py`) |
| XJTU (Wang et al. 2024) | Zenodo 10963339 | `src/xjtu_loader.py` |
| TJU (Zhu et al. 2022) | Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.6405084 (CC BY 4.0) | `src/tju_loader.py`, `scripts/23_build_tju.py` |

Per-cycle feature tables derived from them (`results/*_summary.csv.gz`, `results/xjtu_features.csv.gz`) are included so that the later steps can be run without the raw files; check each dataset's licence before reuse. Large prediction files (`*.npz`) are not included and are regenerated by the scripts.
